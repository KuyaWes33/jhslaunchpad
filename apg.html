Reference

About This Guide

Basis

The Assessment Planning Guide (APG) is anchored on Department Order No. 015 and related assessment guidelines governing the development and administration of classroom assessments.

Purpose

APG assists teachers in planning, reviewing, and improving assessment structures through practical computations and validation rules aligned with established policy requirements.

Assessment Assumptions

  • Term Examination (TE) contributes 40% of the overall assessment.
  • Summative Tests (STs) contribute 60% of the overall assessment.
  • The Term Examination must contain at least 50 items and 50 points.
  • TE Points must be equal to or greater than TE Items.
  • The number of Summative Tests ranges from two (2) to four (4).
  • Each Summative Test must not exceed one-half of the Term Examination points.

Disclaimer

APG is intended solely as a planning aid. It does not replace professional judgment, contextual decision-making, official policy interpretation, or the authority of the Department of Education.
